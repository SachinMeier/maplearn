NYC neighborhood maps

These maps use approximate, colloquial neighborhood boundaries. SoHo, NoHo, Nolita, West Village, and other smaller neighborhoods are separate quiz answers. They are not official administrative districts, and local understandings of their boundaries can differ.

When a small neighborhood overlaps a larger one, the smaller neighborhood gets its own area. The broader name applies to the remaining area: for example, Alphabet City is carved out of East Village. Smaller sub-neighborhoods take precedence, then smaller parent neighborhoods. Parks start green. Airports, the cemetery, and other nonresidential areas are gray. These context areas are not quiz answers.

Data by Chris Whong and NYC Neighborhood Boundaries contributors, derived from Zillow's 2017 neighborhood boundaries. The source snapshot is pinned to commit a14c924b16ce659d034ab15a8f45229b05a1f1f5.
